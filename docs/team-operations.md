# Team Operations

A consistent process helps the team avoid duplicate work and accidental changes. Adapt roles to the number of people available and the current competition rules.

## Suggested roles

- **Team lead:** Tracks time, assigns priorities, resolves conflicts, and communicates with the coach.
- **Image operator:** Makes approved changes and reports results.
- **Reviewer:** Checks evidence, requirements, and likely side effects before changes.
- **Scribe/timekeeper:** Maintains the change log and calls out time checkpoints.

One person may hold multiple roles. Keep a second person aware of significant changes when possible.

## Change log

Keep notes in the team’s approved private location. For each change, record:

| Field | What to record |
| --- | --- |
| Time | When the change was made |
| Finding | What was observed and where |
| Evidence | Why the team believes it needs attention |
| Change | What was changed, at a high level |
| Owner | Who made the change |
| Verification | How the result and required functionality were checked |
| Status | Complete, reverted, or unresolved |

Do not store passwords, flags, answer keys, personal data, or sensitive image contents in this repository.

## Communication rules

- Announce high-impact or uncertain changes before making them.
- State the expected effect and verification method briefly.
- Report failures immediately so the team can avoid repeating the action.
- Escalate conflicts with image instructions to the team lead or coach.
- Leave a clear handoff when switching systems or operators.
