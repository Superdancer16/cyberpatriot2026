# Round Day Checklist

## Before the round

- [ ] Confirm the team understands the current rules and coach instructions.
- [ ] Confirm each participant knows their role and communication channel.
- [ ] Review the image instructions and identify required accounts, services, and applications.
- [ ] Confirm the image and scoring interface are accessible.
- [ ] Open the team’s approved change log.

## During the round

- [ ] Read instructions before making changes.
- [ ] Record the starting score and key requirements.
- [ ] Prioritize evidence-backed findings.
- [ ] Make small, reviewed changes and verify each one.
- [ ] Preserve required functionality and communicate uncertain changes.
- [ ] Check score and remaining time at agreed intervals.
- [ ] Avoid speculative last-minute changes.

## At the end

- [ ] Recheck the image instructions, score, and required functionality.
- [ ] Tell the team lead what is complete and what remains unresolved.
- [ ] Save approved notes in the team’s private location.
- [ ] Record lessons learned without including credentials, flags, or protected materials.
