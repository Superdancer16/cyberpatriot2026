## During the round

Read the image brief, preserve evidence, record baseline scoring and assign work. At the midpoint, ask each operator for current score, blocked items and required services at risk.

## Final review

Stop low-confidence changes at the agreed cutoff. Recheck required access and scoring. Follow submission instructions and record one lesson for the next practice.
