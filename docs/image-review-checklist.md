## Before editing

## Inspect and classify

Group observations into confirmed discrepancy, likely issue, required exception or unknown. Record evidence for each. An unfamiliar service with a required dependency belongs in the exception or unknown column until you verify its purpose.

## Close the review

Confirm authorized sign-in, required service behavior from the intended client, and current scoring status. Record unresolved items and who owns them.
