## How to use this repository

1. Before each round, read the official round instructions and confirm the permitted actions with the coach.
2. Assign team roles and use the workflow to inspect the image before making changes.
3. Record each change, its reason, and its verification in the team’s approved notes.
4. Recheck required services and scoring after each group of changes.
5. After the round, capture lessons learned without recording passwords, flags, personal data, or other secrets.

## Important

The checklists are prompts for investigation, not permission to change every listed setting. Image configurations and scoring requirements vary by round. Do not remove accounts, software, services, or files unless the round instructions and team lead authorize the change. Keep competition materials and credentials private, and follow the current CyberPatriot rules.
